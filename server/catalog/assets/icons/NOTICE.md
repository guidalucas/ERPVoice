# Footer icon sources

Payment brand artwork (`visa.svg`, `mastercard.svg`, `amex.svg`) is vendored without modification from [svg-credit-card-payment-icons](https://github.com/aaronfagan/svg-credit-card-payment-icons), Apache-2.0. See LICENSE-payment-icons.txt.

Mercado Pago, Instagram and WhatsApp marks are vendored without modification from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), CC0-1.0. See LICENSE-simple-icons.txt. Brand names and marks belong to their respective owners.

Generic interface icons are vendored without modification from [Lucide](https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide), ISC. See LICENSE-lucide.txt, including its upstream attribution notices.

These local files are loaded without contacting third-party CDNs at runtime.
